**InnovateHer - Women-Centric Hackathon Initiative**

*As a woman in CS, I founded InnovateHer to create space for others like me. What started as a student government idea became a women-centric hackathon and a community of 400+.*

**Impact:**
- Ran a 36-hour hackathon two years in a row with 300+ participants each year
- Recruited and led 80 organizers and 50 mentors across 7 teams
- 71+ technical projects built and 2 participants landed internships
- Raised $80K: $41,000 in year one and $39,000 in industry partnerships in year two, including Purdue's CS department
- Featured in The Purdue Exponent for its impact on gender diversity in tech
