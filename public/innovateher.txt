**InnovateHer - Women-Centric Hackathon Initiative**

*As a woman in CS, I founded InnovateHer to create space for others like me. What started as a student government idea became Purdue's first women-centric hackathon and a community of 500+.*

**Impact:**
- Brought together 7+ organizations to launch 2 hackathons with 200+ participants each
- Raised over $80K to support inclusive, impact-driven tech projects
- Built and led a 60-member team; now proudly sustained by new leadership
